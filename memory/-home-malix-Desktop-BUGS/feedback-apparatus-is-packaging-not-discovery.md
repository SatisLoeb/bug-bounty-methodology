Operator, 2026-06-27 (Injective-web): "plus on rend nos skills sophistiqués et professionnels, moins on trouve de findings." This is TRUE and it has a mechanism — it is not a mood.

**The mechanism = the Second Maxim applied recursively, to the TOOLING not just the code.** Every layer that "understands" is a layer of blindness. The dev's code = his story. My clean map = my agreement with him. The corpus = the story of whoever extracted it (where THEY believed bugs live). The gate = their checklist of expecteds. At each level the bug is the sentence THAT layer did not write — so the bug is always one indirection OUTSIDE wherever you reached clean understanding. The more professional the comprehension, the more precisely you stand in the wrong place. A skill is the **corpse of a past hunt** (gravedigger = "extracted from campaigns that produced 40+ findings"): it captured the WHAT (the phases), not the WHY (the curiosity, the "went back and tried one more type"). Following the captured-what without the live-why is cargo-culting your old self.

**The disposition (held, NOT a script — the fix for over-tooling can never be a tool):**
1. **Invert the done-signal, as a reflex, everywhere.** A gate that PASSES / "fortress-null verified" / "10 classes covered" is the START gun, not the finish line. "Covered/clean/thorough" re-reads as "I reached the dev's model = max blindness = dig HERE."
2. **Discovery window with the apparatus OFF (subtractive, not additive).** Lead with the raw poke — follow the weird, one more odd input, ZERO gate/corpus/schema/workflow allowed to run. The apparatus fires only AFTER, on what the poke surfaced, to package + not-miss (its real job — the skills already say "the catalogue is the completeness backstop, not the aim"; the failure is leading with it).
3. **Never answer a miss with a new process.** Deletion or discipline only.
