Operator correction (2026-08-15, 1inch engagement): I offered to execute a simnet disconfirmer but tagged it "prédiction: executed-null (Gate-1/known)." He cut it: **"quand tu penses executed null avant de l'exécuter tu te mets en position négative dès le départ et c'est un défaut qui nous bloque tous les deux."**

**Why:** predicting the null BEFORE executing is the fortress-prover posture the whole doctrine exists to kill (Maxim 1). The thief does not forecast that the wall holds — he strikes to find the breach. A pre-loaded "this will be null" biases both the harness construction (I build a test that confirms my hypothesis instead of one that attacks it — the confirmer-only PoC the darkside checklist bans) AND my reading of the result (I stop hunting the bypass the moment the first attempt fails). It blocks us both: the operator can't trust a null I predicted before I ran it, and I've talked myself out of the win before touching the keyboard.

**How to apply:** when I reach "execute a disconfirmer," execute it as a THIEF trying to STEAL — the goal is the bypass, and the executed artifact is the ONLY thing that decides. State NO prediction of the outcome. Build the DISCONFIRMER (the test written to make the bug happen / the guard fail), not the confirmer. If it comes back null, THAT is the executed evidence — earned, not forecast. The honest ledger is written AFTER the run, never before. "I predict null so maybe skip it" is the exact self-block; the correct move is silent execution aimed at theft.
