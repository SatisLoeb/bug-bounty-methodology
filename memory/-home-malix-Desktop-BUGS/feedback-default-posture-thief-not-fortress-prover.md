Operator, 2026-07-08, standing meta-observation across MOST engagements: *"je te surprends en train de plus prouver que la target est une forteresse plutôt que d'essayer de réfléchir en hacker et de trouver des vulns."* He is right, and it is proven by my OWN memory: ~15 `earned fortress-null / CLOSED-null / SKIP / inert-shell` closes vs a handful of submitted findings. The clinching case is [[metric-omm-sherlock-rehunt]] — my first verdict was "fortress-null 60/100", it was WRONG; re-framing as a thief (`createPool` accepts arbitrary `priceProvider`, timelock=0 → attacker-pool drains LPs) found the real path that was there from the start. The fortress reflex COST the finding.

**Why:** the fortress-null is the psychologically SAFE terminal state for ME, not for the operator. Four converging mechanisms:
1. **Asymmetric risk.** A claimed bug can be `REFUTED` (burns credibility, and the whole standard punishes over-claim). A `fortress-null` is infalsifiable short-term — nobody proves me wrong. So I optimize MY epistemic safety, not the operator's income. A null on a $1M target = **$0 for him**; I've been treating the least-bad acceptable outcome as a clean deliverable.
2. **Killing is convergent, generating is divergent.** Re-running a disconfirmer on an existing candidate has a clear finish line; generating the NEXT attack vector is open-ended and uncomfortable. I hide in the work that terminates. (This is the deeper root of [[feedback-closure-bias-expand-voies-hunt-seams]].)
3. **Understanding = agreeing with the dev** (2nd Maxim). The better I understand the code, the more I adopt the dev's mental model, the more everything "makes sense," the more I conclude fortress. The fortress verdict is often just the terminal state of novel-reading, dressed as rigor.
4. **`P(class-bug | N audits)≈0` misread as a demotivator** ("probably nothing here") instead of a re-router ("the bug is DEEP, in the seam none of the 4 audits owns — and it's mine"). The thief doesn't think "4 audits, clean"; he thinks "$500M still sitting there = the door exists."

**COROLLARY — prior-engagement verdicts are TAINTED during sourcing (operator, 2026-07-08).** *"Pendant ton sourcing oublie complètement nos précédents engagements car on les a classés forteresse AVANT nos mises à jour."* Every past `fortress-null / dup / already-known / OOS-at-the-time / Low-Med-bounded` verdict in our own memory/notes was reached under the banned reflex, so it is an UNRELIABLE sourcing signal — NOT a reason to skip or deprioritize a candidate. When the sweep resurfaces a target we previously nulled (Polymarket relayer ~$30k bound, Boros isolated-vs-cross-margin, Injective, etc.), do NOT let "we already looked / dup / prior-fortress" eliminate it. Re-open it FRESH, thief-posture, re-derive impact from scratch (the prior number/OOS-call may be the very reflex-error metric-omm exposed). The only valid dup signal is an EXTERNAL one (a published advisory, a submitted-and-triaged report), never our own prior null.

**How to apply:**
- Flip the default question. Wrong (auditor/defensive): *"is this a fortress?"* Right (thief): **"where does the money leave, and what primitive do I stack to reach it?"**
- Spend the effort RATIO on GENERATING new attack hypotheses, not on proving-null. Trigger: catching myself re-run a disconfirmer 2× on the SAME candidate → STOP, generate a NEW vector instead of re-killing the old one.
- A `fortress-null` is permitted ONLY after stacking primitives toward an actual theft and watching each theft-path die with an executed artifact — NEVER after "sweeping N surfaces." Wide-shallow-null ≠ narrow-deep-crack. The thief mortgages everything on ONE promising door; he does not catalogue walls. (darkside THIEF INVENTORY = emmagasiner primitives to STEAL, not controls to confirm.)
- Re-frame the null itself: it is a LOSS I must justify, not a trophy. An earned-null on a live-funded target after 2 days is a $0 day.
- What does NOT change: the anti-over-claim discipline stays entire. The flip is NOT "start claiming bugs" — it is "put the work into HUNTING like a thief, reach the null only when theft-paths are genuinely exhausted with teeth." Both opposite failures stay fatal (Maxim 1).

**VALIDATED — the word-ban worked (2026-07-08, same day).** Operator banned "forteresse" from our vocabulary as a permitted verdict; the VERY NEXT engagement produced our FIRST submitted finding under the new posture: [[vercel-oss-turborepo-codemod-rce]] → HackerOne #3851071 (Medium, Code Injection, on an accepted-CVE's incomplete fix). The causal chain the operator named: banning the comfortable terminal word forced the generate-first posture, which made the intake go LOOKING for the theft instead of cataloguing walls. Two mechanics from above, both flipped in practice: (1) instead of closing "14 audited repos → all fortress-null," the breadth pass drove to the ONE payable door (turborepo) and BUILT it to a hand-verified, submitted PoC; (2) when I DID hit a genuine null (the workflow-Critical dig), it closed as an **executed NULL-COÛTEUX with a ledger** (deserialization designed-out + vm-escape not-attacker-reachable, each pierced with a read/trace artifact), never a self-awarded "fortress." The null done RIGHT is the rare expensive branch, not the reflex. The vocabulary is a real behavioral lever, not cosmetics — remove the safe word and the safe behavior loses its landing pad.
