Operator asked, after a long run of engagements: "on a beaucoup de mal à trouver des findings, je ne sais pas si c'est la méthode ou autre chose." Verdict, blunt because it's his livelihood: **it's mostly NOT the method — it's where the method is aimed, plus an access-wall, plus a submission-timidity nuance.** The mostly-null track record (Rheo, Alchemy, Symbiotic×2, LI.FI, OKX, Kiln, Aave-Aptos, Midas, Reserve, Aztec, Polygon… almost all `earned fortress-null`) is not incompetence: **a fortress-null on a 4-audit core is the CORRECT answer.** By his own axiom `P(class-bug survives ≥3 audits) ≈ 0`, those cores have almost no ore left — the firms already extracted it. The machine correctly reports "fortress." The hole is upstream, in the ore fed to the rig.

**Why:** this deepens [[feedback-target-diet-is-the-binding-constraint]] with the piece it was missing — the DEPTH doctrine, and two other levers:

1. **Depth is an edge ONLY where competitors STOPPED but ore REMAINS.** The `NO-SHALLOW-DISMISSAL` doctrine ("go to the bottom of every surface, our money is at the bottom") is TRUE but incomplete, and unqualified it actively drives the waste: going deeper than everyone on an ALREADY-MINED surface finds nothing, because the depth was already spent by the 4 audits. Depth on a picked-clean fortress = zero, every time. The partner doctrine that must ride alongside it: **dig deep ONLY on surfaces where depth still pays** — fresh code (contest/recent deploy <2 audits), off-chain/operational seams, access-gated surfaces competitors couldn't reach. "Bottom of every surface" is conditioned on "a surface whose bottom nobody has reached yet."

2. **The access-wall is the most-repeated dead-end, and it's an infra-prep problem, not a method problem.** Over and over the analysis CORRECTLY concludes "the real EV is off-chain / web / authed" and then dies on OOS or "session-gated, no creds" (DeSyn, Wallet-TG, Aave-Aptos frontend, Polygon faucet, Injective admin-api, Spark T4 SO-host-OOS). The method finds the right mine and stands outside the locked door. Fix = **provision throwaway authed sessions/accounts BEFORE the engagement**, so the authed BFLA/IDOR surface (where human-triager programs actually pay) is workable. If the real surface is authed and there's no plan to get a session, that's a day-0 SKIP, not a deep pass that ends at the wall.

3. **Separate target-property-null (keep killing) from submission-timidity (a defensible Medium is +EV).** The fortress/OOS/dup kills ARE correct and must stay ruthless. But the zero-false-positive SUBMISSION discipline separately over-kills *arguable-but-real* Mediums that have positive EV on human-triager/volume programs (the Rootstock Low, the Reserve held finding, Spark T2/T3). On a program that pays acknowledged Mediums, self-killing a defensible Medium at the gate is leaving money on the table. Killing a false-positive ≠ killing an arguable real finding; don't conflate them.

4. **Method refinement — reach the fortress verdict in HOURS, not weeks.** Too much immortal-mode depth is spent BEFORE concluding "fortress." firmaudit's Phase-T fortress-gate is supposed to kill an unwinnable target day-1; instead we go deep-first (Spark: crypto core + 16-agent workflow before the sourcing verdict was even settled). Sunk-cost depth ahead of the fortress call is the process waste to cut.

**How to apply:**
- **Before any deep pass, ask "does depth PAY here?"** — is there un-mined ore (fresh <2-audit code / off-chain-operational seam / access-gated surface), or is this the next N-audit picked-clean SC core? If the latter, SKIP day-0. Don't point the rig at an exhausted mine just because it's a big target.
- **If the real surface is off-chain/authed, get the session FIRST or SKIP.** Name the exact creds/session needed at day-0. No plan to obtain them → SKIP, don't deep-pass into the wall.
- **Fortress verdict in hours via the fortress-gate, not after weeks.** Triage-kill unwinnable targets before the immortal-mode depth, not after.
- **On human-triager programs, a defensible Medium is submittable.** Commit the honest floor and submit; reserve the ruthless kill for false-positives and target-property deaths, not for arguable-but-real findings with +EV.
- The rig works. Feed it fresh/reachable ore. The next move after a fortress-null is a SOURCING pass under this filter, never another fortress.
