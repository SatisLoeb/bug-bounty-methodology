From 2026-06-23, the standing doctrine on every bounty engagement: **we do not let findings be taken.** Two halves, in order.

1. **Surgical BEFORE submission.** Every load-bearing claim executed, not asserted: live re-query for counts/dates, fork or on-chain anchor, the auth/grantee boundary proven (non-privileged → 401), no overclaim, an honest impact bound, verbatim rubric quotes. A claim a triager can falsify in one query — a stale number, an approximate quote, an unproven "any key" — kills the whole report. The dispute downstream is only ever as strong as the verification upstream.

2. **Surgical dispute to the very end.** A wrongful close (downgrade, "duplicate / known internally", Low) is never the verdict — it is a gate to pierce. Re-verify live the day of the close (still exploitable? count grown? still returns 200?), demand the one fact that turns a claim into a fact (the antecedent *date* for a dup; the program's own rubric tier for a severity), anchor on the target's OWN definitions, and carry the contempt in precision, never in an insult — give them nothing to turn against you. Escalate to mediation, then public, when the facts support it. Both failures are fatal: conceding a gate un-exhausted (leaving the diamond in the wall) AND over-claiming a bypass not proven.

**Why:** this session proved both halves. The Injective severity dispute and the Solv `duplicate` appeal were defensible *precisely because* the reports were surgically verified — live LCD re-runs, 6/6 byte-identical sessions, an on-chain tx anchor, grantee-required honesty, zero overclaim, verbatim Cantina/HackenProof rubric text. A sloppy report cannot be defended surgically; a surgical one cannot be quietly dismissed. `Duplicate` / `known internally` is the only verdict in the field that needs zero proof from the issuer, so the move is to force the proof (the date) and put the still-live fact on the record. The resolve to fight to the end is what separates a robbed researcher from a paid one.

**How to apply:** run the kill-gate + preflight on every report before submit — no stale numbers, no approximate quotes, every fact executed by me directly (never trusted from an agent). On every wrongful close: re-verify live first, demand the missing fact, pierce the gate per the First Maxim, escalate calm-factual-unanswerable. See [[feedback-verify-before-working-no-theater]].
