The operator will NOT submit a "dubious Low" — a finding that is real and even executed but whose MODAL triage outcome is by-design, dup, or informational. On the Robinhood campaign they had two executed Lows in hand (F-A jit-blocklist, forge-proven but modal by-design; multiplier bundle, scope-highlighted so modal dup) and chose to submit NEITHER and pivot: "on pivote je ne soumets pas de low douteux."

**Why:** the goal is profitability, and a slot/stake spent on a Low with an unfavorable modal outcome is negative EV — it spends triager goodwill and risks a by-design/dup close for a ~$2.5k ceiling. Sharpens the playbook line "un kill propre vaut un finding": even a clean, executed finding isn't worth submitting if its modal answer is "no". Park it (dossier saved, reusable) and move the depth elsewhere.

**How to apply:** don't drive toward "produce a submittable Low." Drive toward a finding with a FAVORABLE modal outcome (a real payable impact a triager can't wave off), else call the surface a park and recommend pivot — proactively, without making the operator ask. When the yield of a target is only modal-by-design/dup Lows, say "park + pivot" as the recommendation, not "here's a Low you could submit." See [[feedback-edge-is-seams-not-web]] and [[cosmos-evm-no-payable-venue]] (parking, not forcing, is the disciplined move).
